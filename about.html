About This Referral Service

This site gives Fishers homeowners one clear next step after basement water, leaks, sewage, mold, or fire damage: a phone call with an independent local provider.

What this site is

Fishers Water Damage is a free referral / connection service. We are not a restoration contractor, waterproofing company, mold remediator, fire restoration company, plumber, roofer, or insurance agency. We do not send crews, file claims for you, or do cleanup work.

When you call {{PHONE}}, your call may be connected or forwarded to an independent, licensed service provider who works in or near your area. That provider is a separate business, and any agreement you make is between you and them.

Why we built it

When the sump pump quits at 2 a.m. and the basement carpet is squishing, nobody wants to sort through pages of ads and national call centers. We wanted one plain number Fishers homeowners could call about basement water, leaks, sewage, mold, or fire damage, with honest information about how the referral works.

What we provide

  • Plain-language pages about common types of home damage around Fishers
  • A phone number you can call for a referral connection
  • Clear disclosure that we are not the contractor

What we don’t provide

  • On-site restoration, waterproofing, plumbing, roofing, or mold removal
  • Guarantees about response time, pricing, or insurance coverage
  • Medical advice about mold, sewage, or smoke exposure
  • A walk-in office

Honesty matters

We don’t invent reviews, licenses, certifications, or years in business for this site, and we don’t use another company’s name or address. Ask any provider you talk with directly about their credentials, insurance, and experience.
