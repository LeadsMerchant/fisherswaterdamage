Why basements around Fishers take on water

It is not just bad luck. The ground here holds water.

Clay soil and high groundwater

Hamilton County sits on dense glacial clay. Neighboring Carmel’s engineering department notes that most local soils drain very poorly and have seasonal high water tables, so basements commonly flood when the ground is saturated.

Sump pumps do the heavy lifting

Many Fishers basements depend on a sump pump to move groundwater away. When the pump wears out, the float sticks, or the power goes out, water rises fast.

Spring and summer storms

Central Indiana thunderstorms can bring heavy rain and wind at the same time, often knocking out power right when a sump pump is needed most.

Finished basements

Carpet, drywall, and a basement bedroom or office turn a little water into a big cleanup, and they can hide moisture until it smells musty.

Rivers and the reservoir

The White River forms Fishers’ western edge and Geist Reservoir borders the east side, with Fall Creek feeding it. Homes near the water can check flood zones on FEMA’s map service.

Freeze and thaw

Indiana winters bring hard freezes. Pipes in exterior walls, garages, and crawl spaces can split, and the leak often shows up when it warms back up.

Sources: Hamilton County Soil & Water Conservation District, “Building With the Land”; City of Carmel engineering FAQ on basement flooding; City of Fishers mapping.

Frequently asked questions

Are you a water damage company?

No. This is a free referral line. We try to connect you with an independent, licensed provider who handles your type of problem, from a wet basement to sewage, mold, or fire and smoke damage.

My sump pump failed during a storm. Can I call at night?

Yes, call any time. Many restoration companies run emergency crews after hours, and we will try to reach one that serves your address. We cannot guarantee a provider is available at every hour.

Which areas do you cover?

Fishers (ZIP codes 46037, 46038, and the Fishers part of 46040) plus nearby Noblesville, Carmel, McCordsville, Fortville, Ingalls, and the northeast side of Indianapolis. Coverage depends on which providers are available when you call.

What kinds of problems can I call about?

Flooded or wet basements, sump pump failures, storm and roof leaks, burst or frozen pipes, sewage backups through floor drains, mold after water damage, and fire or smoke damage.

Does it cost anything to call?

No. There is no charge to call. Any inspection or repair costs are between you and the independent provider you decide to hire.

Will my insurance cover a wet basement?

It depends on your policy and the cause. Many standard policies only cover sump pump or drain backup if you added that coverage, and outside flooding usually needs separate flood insurance. Check with your agent.
